O. Reg 369/09 — Power-Assisted Bicycles

Strict Legal Criteria
  • Continuous Power: Max 500 Watts rated power at motor shaft.
  • Top Speed Cap: Hard capped at 32 km/h on level ground motor power alone.
  • Weight Boundary: Total unladen mass cannot exceed 120 kg.
  • Functional Pedals: Must have permanently affixed pedals. If removed or unusable, it defaults to a Motor Vehicle.
  • Bilingual Label: Permanent manufacturer label stating in English and French that it is a "Power-Assisted Bicycle / Bicyclette Assistée".

Motor Vehicle Classification Laws

Fallback Status

Any motorized vehicle that fails to meet even one criterion under O. Reg 369/09 automatically stops being classified as a bicycle and legally defaults to a Motor Vehicle.

Roadside Requirements
  • CAIA s. 2(1): Compulsory Financial Responsibility (Insurance) is mandatory.
  • HTA s. 32(1): Operator must hold a valid driver's licence (Class M, M2, or LSM).
  • HTA s. 7(1): Requires dynamic registration permit and a physical rear plate.
